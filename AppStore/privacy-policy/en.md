# Privacy Policy — Chess

*Last updated: September 8, 2026*

## In short

Chess does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## In detail

**We collect no data.** The app never asks for your name, email address, phone
number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

**It does not connect to the internet.** Chess contains no networking code. The
chess engine is the app's own and runs on your device, the pieces are drawn from
vector paths in code and the sounds are generated at launch, so the game works
entirely offline.

**No analytics, no advertising.** The app contains no ad networks, no analytics
frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere. It
links only Apple's own frameworks.

**No in-app purchases.** The app contains no in-app purchases and no
subscriptions. Nothing in it is for sale, and no feature — the game review
included — is limited by a quota or held back for payment.

**Where your progress is stored.** Your settings, the game you have in progress,
the last finished game and which chapters of the guide you have read are stored
solely on your device using the system UserDefaults store. This data never
leaves the device. It is included in your regular device backup (iCloud or
computer) if you have one enabled, in which case Apple's own policies apply.
Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

**Device access.** The app does not use the camera, microphone, location
services, contacts, health data or any other sensitive permission. It only uses
haptic feedback and audio playback.

**Children.** The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for any age. Because the app
collects no data, no data about anyone of any age is created.

## Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
