A big update: timed games, variants, puzzles, training and an archive of your games.

• An adaptive opponent that grows stronger as you do, and premoves while the computer thinks
• Timed games from bullet to classical, or minutes and increment of your own; on the clock the computer budgets its own time
• A chess clock for a game on a real board — increment, simple delay or Bronstein, and time odds
• Chess960, King of the Hill, Three-check, and the classical pawn, knight, rook and queen odds
• Face to face: lay the device between two players and each side's pieces face their player
• About 4,000 puzzles built in: rated puzzles, 32 themes, a daily puzzle with a streak and a home-screen widget, races against the clock, and your own mistakes to fix
• Training: an opening trainer, an analysis board, a position editor, and pasting in any PGN or FEN
• My Games: an archive of your finished games, statistics, a rough strength estimate, 31 achievements, and PGN import and export
• Game Review now shows the time spent on every move, and judges repeated positions and moves that walk into mate more accurately
